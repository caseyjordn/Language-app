力
Power
Vocabulary · Lv. 4

Meaning

Primary
Power
Alternatives
Strength, Ability
Word Type
Noun

When a vocab word is made up of a single kanji and that's it, it almost always takes the meaning from its parent kanji. So, this vocab word also means power.

Reading

ちから

When a vocab word is a single kanji standing alone like this, it usually uses the kun'yomi reading. Mnemonic: the martial art that requires the most power is chee-se kara-te (ちから). Practitioners channel all their power into karate-chopping blocks of cheese.

Context

Common Word Combinations
力がつく — to gain strength
力が入る — to be filled with strength
Context Sentences
力の大きさが、ちがう。
The size of the forces is different.
うちではママが大きな力をもってるんですよ。
At my house, my mom has a lot of power.
